## What the bio must not say

No supply figure, no fee percentage, no market cap, no split. Those are
constants in a verified contract — that is where they are binding, and a bio
cannot be checked against the chain. A number in a bio is a claim with nothing
behind it, and it is the first thing that goes stale.

Also skip: "revolutionary", "the future of", "100x", "safu", "based", anything
implying a price will go up, and anything implying an audit that has not
happened.
